The Balloon That Clings to a Wall

physics experimentGuide: Julius Sumner Miller · historical science educator

Can an ordinary balloon stay on a wall without glue? Change one variable and record how long it clings.

What it demonstrates

Frictional contact transfers charge between some materials. A charged balloon polarizes the nearby wall surface, creating an electrostatic attraction.

Open experiment instructions

Materials

  • An inflated balloon
  • A wool scarf or sweater
  • A dry, clean wall
  • A timer

Procedure

  1. Rub the balloon against wool for five strokes.
  2. Gently place the rubbed side against the wall and release it.
  3. Measure how many seconds it remains on the wall.
  4. Repeat with ten strokes, keeping other conditions similar. Compare the times.

Safety: Keep loose balloon pieces away from young children; avoid latex if allergic.
