Pendulum: Does Length Change the Rhythm?

physics experimentGuide: Ada · inventive physics explorer

Build two simple pendulums and measure how length affects their swing time.

What it demonstrates

For a small-angle simple pendulum, the period grows approximately with the square root of its length. The bob mass has little effect under ideal conditions.

Open experiment instructions

Materials

  • String
  • Two small identical washers
  • Ruler
  • Stopwatch
  • Secure low support

Procedure

  1. Tie one washer to a 30 cm string on a stable support.
  2. Pull it aside by a small angle and release without pushing.
  3. Time ten complete swings; divide by ten.
  4. Repeat at 60 cm length, keeping the release angle and washer the same.

Safety: Use small, lightweight washers, keep the swinging path clear, and avoid overhead installation.
