Walking Water Without Lifting the Cup

physics experimentGuide: Professor Willow · fictional experiment host

Can water climb paper and move into a neighboring cup?

What it demonstrates

Capillary action carries water through tiny pores in paper towels. Adhesion, cohesion and surface tension contribute to this transport.

Open experiment instructions

Materials

  • Two clear cups
  • Water
  • A strip of paper towel
  • Food coloring (optional)

Procedure

  1. Fill one cup halfway and leave the second empty.
  2. Fold the towel into a narrow strip.
  3. Place one end in the water and the other in the empty cup.
  4. Check every 15 minutes and record water levels.

Safety: Place cups on a spill-proof tray. Do not drink the experimental water.
