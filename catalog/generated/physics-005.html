Paper Airplane: A Study in Drag

physics experimentGuide: Captain Nova · fictional test pilot

Measure how a small wing change affects distance rather than guessing which plane is fastest.

What it demonstrates

Drag is the air resistance opposite the direction of motion. Changing wing flaps can increase drag and alter a paper aircraft’s flight.

Open experiment instructions

Materials

  • Several sheets of identical paper
  • Ruler
  • Tape to mark a start line
  • Large clear indoor area

Procedure

  1. Fold two matching paper airplanes.
  2. Throw the first five times from the same line, recording distance.
  3. Fold small symmetric flaps up on the second plane.
  4. Throw five times using the same technique and compare average distances.

Safety: Keep the test lane free of people; do not point planes toward faces.
